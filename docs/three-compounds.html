Overlapping the assembly onto the pitch is what makes this work

Three separate assembly areas plus three separate pitches needs 2,359 m² at dense rates. Overlapped — the same paved surface, used at different times of day — it needs 1,424 m². That single decision releases 935 m², which is the difference between a scheme that fits and one that does not.

Why primary takes the south and kindergarten the north

The plot tapers: the south band is 68 m wide, the north only 53. Primary is the hungriest compound — 1,750 students need a 630 m² yard on top of the largest building — so it has to take the widest band. Kindergarten needs the least of everything and fits the narrowest. The allocation is forced by the site's geometry, not chosen. It flips only if the road hierarchy says the youngest children must sit furthest from the traffic, and that answer is still outstanding.

One correction carried into this plan: the secondary building holds a 500 m² minimum floor plate. Sized purely on area it would come out 15 m long over seven storeys — a point block where the stair and lift eat the floor. At five levels and 24 m long it behaves like a building.

The budget — and where the compliant schedule fails

Both programmes measured against the same 4,618 m² of ground, with every building already at its maximum permitted height — kindergarten G+2, primary and secondary G+6. The black line is the site boundary.

The compliant schedule needs 4,740 m² against a 4,618 m² site. There is no height left to trade — every volume is already at the cap. So this is not a massing problem that better planning solves.

The decision this forces

You cannot have three separated compounds and the guideline-compliant schedule on this plot. One of the two has to give, and until it does, nothing downstream — plans, structure, cost — can be committed. Three ways to close 122 m² are below, in the order I would try them.

05

The question I still need answered

Where does the central office go?

The 213 m² group function — managing director, principal, academic director, finance, HR, records — serves all three schools. If staff cannot intermingle either, it cannot sit inside any one compound without that school's staff hosting the other two. My suggestion is a small fourth volume at the main gate: it solves visitor reception for all three without anyone entering a school, and it gives the academy a public face on the road. The alternative is to put it in one compound with its own separate external entrance.

Everything else I can now proceed on. Still open from earlier rounds, and unchanged: which boundary is the Ambo Road, the road reservation setbacks, and four boreholes inside the site polygon.
